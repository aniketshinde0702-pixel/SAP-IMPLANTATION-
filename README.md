# SAP FICO Internship Project

## 📌 Overview

This project documents hands-on **SAP FICO (Financial Accounting & Controlling)** configuration and transaction activities performed during an internship.
It covers core **FI modules** including **Enterprise Structure, General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Asset Accounting, GST, and TDS** with real-time business scenarios.

---

## 🏢 Modules Covered

### 1️⃣ Enterprise Structure

* Define Company & Company Code
* Assign Company Code to Company
* Field Status Variant
* Fiscal Year Variant
* Posting Period Configuration

---

### 2️⃣ General Ledger Accounting (FI-GL)

**Configuration**

* Chart of Accounts
* Account Groups
* Retained Earnings
* Tolerance Groups

**Transactions**

* G/L Master (FS00)
* Journal Entries (F-02, FB50)
* Park & Post Documents
* G/L Balance & Trial Balance (FS10N, F.08)

---

### 3️⃣ Accounts Payable (FI-AP)

**Configuration**

* Vendor Account Groups
* Number Ranges
* AP Reconciliation Account
* Payment Terms
* House Bank & APP

**Transactions**

* Vendor Master (FK01/FK02)
* Vendor Invoice (FB60)
* Vendor Payment (F-53)
* Down Payment & Clearing (F-48, F-54)

---

### 4️⃣ Accounts Receivable (FI-AR)

**Configuration**

* Customer Account Groups
* Number Ranges
* AR Reconciliation Account

**Transactions**

* Customer Master (FD01/FD02)
* Sales Invoice (FB70)
* Customer Receipt (F-28)
* Advance & Clearing
* Dunning Process (FBMP, F150)

---

### 5️⃣ Tax on Sales & Purchases (GST)

* GST Configuration (CGST, SGST, IGST)
* Tax Procedures & Condition Types
* Tax Codes (FTXP)
* Purchase & Sales Tax Posting

---

### 6️⃣ Asset Accounting (FI-AA)

* Chart of Depreciation
* Asset Classes
* Depreciation Methods:

  * Straight Line Method (SLM)
  * Written Down Value (WDV)
* Asset Master & Sub-Asset (AS01, AS11)

---

### 7️⃣ TDS / Withholding Tax

* Extended Withholding Tax Configuration
* Withholding Tax Types & Codes
* TDS Payable G/L Account
* Assignment to Vendor Master

---
